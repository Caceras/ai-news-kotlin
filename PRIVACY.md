# AI Brief Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

AI Brief (Android package `com.caceras.aibrief`) is published by Caceras. Contact for privacy, editorial corrections and support: rikicaceras@gmail.com.

## Reader accounts and permissions

AI Brief does not require an account. It does not request location, contacts, microphone, camera, advertising ID, or access to shared files. The Play-compatible edition uses the normal Internet permission. It has no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not generate content from reader prompts.

## Your device

Saved articles, their text, cached editions, and the list of stories you have opened (used to mark them as read), and the story text size you choose are kept in private app storage on your device. Search is performed on your device; search queries are not sent to a search service. Article images may be cached in private app storage. Android cloud backup is disabled for app data.

We do not upload your bookmarks, reading history, or search queries. Local data remains until you remove a saved article, tap Clear reading history on the About page, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings, uninstall the app, or the system removes a cache. These actions do not delete copies of our public articles from the publication.

## Network requests and hosting

The launch edition downloads the public editorial edition from GitHub’s raw content hosting over HTTPS. If a story has a cover image, the app requests it from the image URL chosen by the publisher. The bundled illustrative artwork needs no image request. The Play-compatible edition does not request third-party RSS feeds.

Normal Internet requests disclose connection information to the receiving host, including the requesting IP address, the requested URL, the request time, and an app user-agent. Hosting providers may retain this information for content delivery, reliability, security and abuse prevention under their own policies. We do not use these requests to build reader profiles or to show targeted advertising. Provider retention is controlled by the provider, not by clearing app storage.

GitHub’s privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

The separate direct-install test edition can also check GitHub Releases for app updates and download a new test APK. This self-update feature and its package-install permission are excluded from the Play-compatible edition. It sends no saved articles or search queries.

## Links, sharing and email

Source, contact and privacy links open in your device’s browser. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies. Sharing opens Android’s share sheet and sends the text you choose to the app you select. Contacting the editor opens your email app; sending a message shares your address and message with the recipient through your chosen email service.

Support correspondence may be kept as needed to answer your request and resolve the reported issue. To request deletion of correspondence we control, contact the email below. We cannot delete an email provider’s or hosting provider’s independent records.

## Security, retention and deletion

The app disallows cleartext HTTP traffic and uses private Android app storage. No reader account is created, so there is no reader account to delete. To delete local app data, use Android Settings > Apps > AI Brief > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall. Requests relating to support correspondence can be sent to rikicaceras@gmail.com.

## Changes and contact

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the Play Console declarations must be updated. Questions or correction requests: rikicaceras@gmail.com. Publisher and contact details: https://github.com/Caceras/ai-news-kotlin/blob/main/CONTACT.md

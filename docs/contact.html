AI Brief publishes original editorial writing and explainers about artificial intelligence. Stories show their publication date, publisher and byline. Source links let readers check the underlying references.

Contact the editor

For support, corrections, source suggestions, privacy questions or rights concerns, email rikicaceras@gmail.com. Include the story title and describe the issue so we can review it.

Editorial practice

The launch edition includes AI-assisted explainers. Illustrative AI-generated artwork is labeled in the article reader and is not presented as a photograph of a reported event. New articles are published by the repository’s authorized editors. Readers cannot submit public posts or generate content inside the app.

We link to primary sources and distinguish explainers from current reporting. Editorial corrections are made by updating the affected article. Saved copies on readers’ devices can remain until they remove them.

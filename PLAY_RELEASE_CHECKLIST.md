# Play Release Checklist

## Before V3 Submission

- Host `PRIVACY.md` at a public, stable HTTPS URL and add the same URL to Play Console and the in-app privacy screen.
- Publish a contact page at that URL or another public HTTPS URL with the support email or phone number shown in the app. Use that same contact URL in the News and Magazine declaration.
- Complete the News and Magazine declaration with current publisher, source, contact, and editorial details.
- Complete Data Safety accurately: the app has no account, analytics, ads, or data sale; saved reads remain on-device.
- Declare no ads, complete the target audience and content-rating questionnaires, and add the current privacy-policy URL.
- Upload the signed Android App Bundle, provide phone screenshots, a 512 by 512 store icon, and a feature graphic.
- Run a closed test, verify live-feed refresh and source links, then review Android vitals before production rollout.
